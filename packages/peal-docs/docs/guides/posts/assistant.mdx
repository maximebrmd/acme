The assistant drafts and reworks a post's text in the composer, and can make an image for it.

## Ask the writing assistant [#assistant]

When the workspace has its own [text provider](/guides/workspace#text-provider), or the Peal instance has an AI provider, the composer's **Text** section has an **Assistant** menu. It works on the text for all channels.

| Action                | What it does                                                                                                     |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Write a post…**     | Say what the post is about, in your own words, and the assistant writes a first text, in the subject's language. |
| **Rewrite in a tone** | Professional, friendly, casual, enthusiastic or witty, keeping the meaning, the language and about the length.   |
| **Shorten to fit**    | One of the platforms of the channels you picked, or, before you pick any, each platform whose limit Peal knows.  |
| **Translate into**    | English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Japanese or Chinese (simplified).          |
| **Suggest hashtags**  | Up to 8 that the text does not have yet.                                                                         |

About **Write a post…**:

- Choose a tone if you like, and, when the post goes to a platform with a short limit (X, Bluesky, Threads or Mastodon), the one whose limit it must fit.
- It is offered whether the text is empty or not, and using its text replaces the one you had.
- It is told not to add facts, figures, names, dates or links that the subject does not give, and to add hashtags only when the subject asks for them.
- For another idea, ask again: each one is a request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Open **Assistant** and choose what it should do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the suggestion">
    The suggestion opens in a preview, and nothing changes yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it or drop it">
    Choose **Use this text**, or **Add these hashtags**, which adds them on a
    line of their own. **Discard** drops it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Read the suggestion before you use it. The assistant keeps mentions, hashtags
  and links, and is told not to add facts, but it can still get things wrong.
</Callout>

## Images [#images]

When the workspace has its own [image provider](/guides/workspace#image-provider), or the instance has an image model, **Choose from the library** offers **Generate an image**. It makes an image from a description into the library, picked for the post: see [Generate an image](/guides/media#generate).

An image from the instance's model costs 5 of the day's requests; one from the workspace's own provider costs none.

## Limits [#limits]

| Limit                                  | Value                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Text or subject                        | Up to 5,000 characters                                                                                                 |
| Requests a day                         | 100 per workspace unless the instance sets another, counted from midnight UTC. The menu shows how many are left today. |
| With the workspace's own text provider | None counts: the menu says the workspace's provider writes, and that provider takes up to 500 requests a day.          |

- A request that could not reach the AI provider does not count.
- One that took more than 90 seconds does, as the provider may have worked on it.
- The requests are counted for each workspace, never for each person: every member shares the workspace's day, and each workspace a [partner](/developers/embedding) owns has its own.

## Where your text goes [#privacy]

Your text, or the subject, goes to the workspace's own text provider when it set one, and otherwise to the AI provider the instance's administrator chose, and nowhere else. Peal saves neither the text you send nor the suggestion, and never writes them to its logs.
