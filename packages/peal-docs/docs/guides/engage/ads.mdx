Connect a workspace to its ad accounts on Meta, see what their ads did beside what your posts did on their own, and boost a post you published through Peal as an ad on Facebook or Instagram.

<Callout type="note" title="New">
  This feature is new: its API may still grow. It is off unless the operator of
  the instance turned it on, and an instance may offer reading without spending.
  Nothing spends until an owner allows an ad account to, with a monthly ceiling.
</Callout>

## What works today [#what-works]

- Connect Meta ad accounts, through a sign-in of its own, apart from the one that connects Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts as [channels](/guides/channels).
- Read each connected ad account's campaigns, ad sets and ads, those made outside Peal included, and what each ad did each day.
- See organic and paid side by side: a published post's own counts on each channel, beside what the ads that promote it did.
- [Boost a published post](/guides/engage/boosts), on a Facebook Page, or on an Instagram account connected through its Page, with a budget, an end date and an audience.
- Pause, resume and stop a boost.
- Notifications to the workspace's owners about every boost, [webhook events](/developers/ads#webhooks) for your own systems, and an [audit trail](/guides/engage/boosts#audit) of every decision about money.

The [API](/developers/ads#api), the SDKs, the [command line and MCP tools](/developers/ads#clients) do the same. [What is not built](#not-built) lists the rest.

## Who can do what [#access]

Ads have four permissions. A member holds those of their [role](/guides/posts#roles):

| Permission      | What it allows                                                                                                                        | Who holds it               |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| `ads:read`      | See the ad accounts, their campaigns and numbers, a post's paid results, and the boosts                                               | Owners, admins and editors |
| `ads:manage`    | Sign in to an ad network, connect and disconnect ad accounts, and pause or stop a boost                                               | Owners and admins          |
| `ads:spend`     | Boost a post, resume a paused boost, and pause or stop one                                                                            | Owners and admins          |
| `ads:authorize` | Decide whether an ad account may spend through Peal, set its monthly ceiling, and read the [audit trail](/guides/engage/boosts#audit) | Owners only                |

Some of it is done by a person only, signed in to Peal in their own session, never by an API key, an application or staff acting as a member:

- signing in to the ad network and choosing the ad accounts to connect (`ads:manage`), and only who signed in picks accounts from their sign-in;
- allowing an ad account to spend, and setting or changing its ceiling (`ads:authorize`).

An [API key](/guides/workspace#api-keys) does what the ads permissions it was given allow, `ads:authorize` never among them. An OAuth application needs the scope `workspace:ads` too, which opens nothing alone: beside `workspace:write` for what `ads:manage` allows an application, beside `workspace:publish` for `ads:spend` ([What each permission allows](/developers/agents#scopes)). Staff acting as a member only read.

## Connect ad accounts [#connect]

In the web app, open a workspace, then **Ads**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Meta">
    An owner or an admin chooses to connect Meta, and signs in on Meta's page.
    Peal asks to read ads. On an instance that offers spending, it also asks for
    what making and running ads takes, which the person may decline: the
    accounts are then read, and cannot boost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the ad accounts">
    Back in Peal, the sign-in lists the ad accounts it reaches, each with its
    currency, time zone, status and spend cap. Nothing is connected yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the accounts">
    They tick the accounts the workspace uses. Up to 25 are connected at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A sign-in is completed once, within 15 minutes, by who started it. One that connected no account is removed after a day. Peal stores the sign-in's access token encrypted, and never shows it.

Spending is off on every newly connected account. An account whose time zone Peal does not know cannot be connected.

When the network refuses a sign-in's token, its accounts show that they need reconnecting, and the members who manage ad accounts are [told](/guides/engage/boosts#notices). Nothing is read of those accounts, nor boosted there, until the person who signed in does it again.

### Disconnect [#disconnect]

- Disconnecting an ad account removes what Peal read of it.
- Removing a sign-in removes its token and every account it connects.
- Their boosts' records stay with the workspace, and what they committed in a month still counts against the ceiling when the same account is connected again.
- What runs at the network that Peal did not make is left as it is.

<Callout type="warning" title="Stop running boosts first">
  Both are refused while a boost of theirs runs, may run, is paused or is being
  made (`AdAccountBusy`), as a paused campaign can run again: stop it first.
</Callout>

## What Peal reads [#reading]

Every hour, Peal reads each connected ad account from its network: its campaigns, ad sets and ads, and each ad's numbers for each day of the account's calendar.

| Number      | What it is                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spend       | What the ad cost, in the account's [minor units](/guides/engage/boosts#money)                           |
| Impressions | How often it was shown                                                                                  |
| Reach       | How many people saw it that day. Summed over several days, someone seen on several days counts each day |
| Clicks      | Clicks on the ad                                                                                        |
| Results     | Reactions, comments and shares the network counts on the post. Missing, not zero, when it reported none |

- The first read of an account takes the last 90 days. Later ones read the last 7 days again, as networks correct recent days for a while.
- A read stops once the account reaches 75% of the rate limit its network gives it, and goes on at a later hour: Peal never uses an ad account's whole allowance.
- A day of numbers is kept 396 days (13 months), so a month can be compared with the same one a year before.
- Numbers are the network's own, read with a delay of up to an hour or more. They are not an invoice: what the network bills is in the ad account.

Peal keeps counts and money. It never reads who saw an ad.

### Organic and paid for a post [#post-performance]

A published post's page shows two sides:

- its organic counts on each channel, as [Analytics](/guides/engage/analytics) last read them;
- what the ads that promote it did, by ad account, with its boosts. An ad made outside Peal that promotes the same post is counted too.

The two are never added together: what a platform counts on a post already includes what its ads brought. Amounts in different currencies are never added either, so paid totals come one per ad account.

The organic side is empty for someone who may not read analytics, and for a platform whose counts Peal [does not read](/guides/engage/analytics#schedule), such as Facebook Pages.

## Your data [#data]

- Deleting a workspace deletes its sign-ins with their tokens, its ad accounts, what was read of them, its boosts, their journal and its audit trail. It is refused while a boost runs, may run or is being made: stop it first.
- Closing your account removes your id from what stays with the workspaces you shared. The sign-ins you made stay with their ad accounts, without it, until the account is disconnected or the workspace deleted.
- The [copy of your data](/guides/workspace/account#export) holds, for each workspace you are the only member of, an `ads` object: its sign-ins without their tokens, its ad accounts, its boosts and its audit trail.
- When someone asks Meta to delete what an instance holds about them, Peal removes the sign-ins they made and the ad accounts these connect. When they remove the app from their Meta account, their sign-ins are marked to renew.

What an ad needs goes to the ad network: the post's id, the budget, the dates, the audience, and the beneficiary and payer.

## What is not built [#not-built]

- Other ad networks than Meta's.
- Creating campaigns, ad sets or ads with creatives of their own: Peal boosts a post that is already published.
- Boosting a post that was not published through Peal, or one on an Instagram account connected with Instagram Login.
- Changing a running boost's budget or audience: stop it, and make another.
- Interests, custom audiences, gender, or locations finer than countries.
- Special ad categories: housing, employment, financial products and services, social issues, elections or politics.
- Conversions, lead forms and automated rules.
