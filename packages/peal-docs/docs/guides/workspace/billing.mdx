On Peal Cloud, each workspace is on its [billing account](#accounts)'s plan: the free tier, or per-account pricing once a payment method is added.

Both have every platform, the calendar, analytics, the API, the [MCP server](/developers/mcp) and the [command line](/developers/cli), with members, workspaces and posts unlimited. A server you run yourself bills nothing and limits nothing ([Self-hosting](/self-hosting/options#billing)).

## Per-account pricing [#pricing]

A billing account pays for the social media accounts connected across all its workspaces, on one graduated ladder, per month: the month's accounts, counted by the day as below, are priced band by band. Each price is shown excluding VAT and with France's {{vat-rate}}% VAT, in US dollars.

| Accounts                                           | A month, per account   |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| {{accounts-band-1-from}} to {{accounts-band-1-to}} | {{accounts-band-1-en}} |
| {{accounts-band-2-from}} to {{accounts-band-2-to}} | {{accounts-band-2-en}} |
| {{accounts-band-3-from}} to {{accounts-band-3-to}} | {{accounts-band-3-en}} |
| {{accounts-band-4-from}} and up                    | {{accounts-band-4-en}} |

Accounts are counted by the day:

- Each UTC day of the month counts the most accounts connected at once that day, across the billing account, for days 1 to {{days-per-month}} only (day {{day-not-counted}} is never counted).
- At the end of the month the account-days are added up and divided by {{days-per-month}}, and that number of accounts, which may hold a fraction, goes through the ladder: the first {{free-account-days}} account-days are free.
- An account you disconnect stops counting from the next day, and nothing else is charged for accounts: no fee of any kind.
- A channel that is turned off or needs reconnecting still counts; an ad account does not.
- On the first of each month, the invoice bills the month past.

With every account connected all month:

| -------------------------- | -------------------- |

| {{accounts-5-count-en}}    | {{accounts-5-en}}    |
| {{accounts-10-count-en}}   | {{accounts-10-en}}   |
| {{accounts-20-count-en}}   | {{accounts-20-en}}   |
| {{accounts-50-count-en}}   | {{accounts-50-en}}   |
| {{accounts-100-count-en}}  | {{accounts-100-en}}  |
| {{accounts-1000-count-en}} | {{accounts-1000-en}} |

A month not held in full: {{part-month-all-en}} accounts connected all month, plus {{part-month-extras-en}}, come to {{part-month-days-en}} account-days. Divided by {{days-per-month}}, that is {{part-month-units-en}} accounts through the ladder: {{part-month-en}} for the month.

Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT. The VAT you pay depends on your country: it is added at checkout for customers in the EU, at their country's rate. A business with a valid VAT number pays the price excluding VAT, the VAT being reverse-charged; in France, French VAT applies.

At checkout you can pay in your own currency, converted by our payment provider at the day's rate; otherwise your card is charged in US dollars. Invoices are in US dollars.

The workspace's **Billing** page shows its plan and how much of each limit it uses. `GET /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/billing` answers them too.

## Usage meters [#meters]

What costs Peal by use has a meter, with a monthly allowance per billing account across all its workspaces, from the first of each calendar month (UTC).

| Meter                                                                          | Included each month       | Past the allowance                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Messages sent from the [inbox](/guides/engage/inbox), comment replies included | {{messages-allowance-en}} | {{messages-price-en}} for {{messages-per-en}}, billed by the message |
| [Managed ads](/guides/engage/ads), each ad active in the month                 | {{ads-allowance-en}}      | {{ads-price-en}} per ad                                              |
| The writing assistant's requests and images, on the instance's models          | coming soon               | coming soon                                                          |
| [X](/platforms/x)'s API: posts sent and, where turned on, counts read          | nothing                   | at X's rates ([below](#x))                                           |

Without a payment method, a meter pauses at its allowance until the first of the next month, while reading, analytics and publishing go on. With one, each unit past the allowance is billed on the month's invoice. While a payment has failed, the allowances still apply and nothing past them is used until it goes through. Until a billing account's first invoice is paid, what its meters bill is capped, whatever the ceilings, the months since its subscription began included.

Past what a meter allows, the action is refused and nothing is counted: the API answers 402 `UsageAllowanceReached`, with the `meter`, what it `allowed`, what was `used`, when it `resetsAt` and the `reason` ([Errors](/developers/conventions/errors)).

The writing assistant on the instance's models needs a payment method. With one, its requests and its images each have a monthly allowance per billing account, which the billing page shows; until their prices are set, nothing past it is billed, and the assistant stops there until the next month. A generation that fails once the provider had it is not counted, up to the allowance. A workspace's own [image](/guides/workspace#image-provider) and [text](/guides/workspace#text-provider) providers cost nothing, on any plan.

### X, at X's rates [#x]

X bills each call to its API, and Peal passes it on at X's published pay-per-use rates, with nothing added. A billing account needs a payment method before it connects an X account, and X's use is billed on its monthly invoice, up to a ceiling of {{x-ceiling-en}} excluding tax a month unless its billing managers change it on the billing page.

| A call                                      | X's rate           |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| A post sent, each part of a thread          | {{x-post-en}}      |
| A post sent with a link                     | {{x-post-link-en}} |
| A post's counts read, once a UTC day        | {{x-post-read-en}} |
| An account's followers read, once a UTC day | {{x-user-read-en}} |

The rates are those X's developer pricing page gave on {{x-rates-read-en}}; X may change them, and the billing page shows those billed. Reading counts is off until a member who manages billing turns it on for the workspace, on the billing page ([Analytics](/guides/engage/analytics)). Before each part of a thread goes out, the meter is checked for room for every part still to go; a thread with no room from its first part is not sent at all, and at the ceiling, posts to X fail without being sent (`xCeilingReached`) and counts wait for the next month, while every other platform goes on; billing managers are told at 80% and at 100% of it. An X post fails the same way when the billing account has no payment method (`paymentMethodRequired`), its last payment failed (`paymentFailed`) or it is on a plan from before per-account pricing (`perAccountPricingRequired`); a thread cut part way fails as `xThreadCut`, its first parts live: room the check found was taken by another claim before a later part (another post or X channel of the account, X's reads) or the account changed meanwhile, as the check is not a hold. Reads X does not return (a refusal, a deleted post) are not billed. A server you run yourself bills nothing for X: its operator's settings decide ([Environment variables](/self-hosting/environment-variables)).

## A payment method [#payment-method]

Ordering per-account pricing ([Ordering](#ordering)) adds a payment method to the billing account, and moves it off the free tier:

| What                                        | Without a payment method                       | With a payment method                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connected accounts                          | The first {{free-accounts}}, and no more       | Any number, billed by the ladder                                                  |
| Meters                                      | Each stops at its allowance                    | Use past the allowance is billed                                                  |
| Writing assistant, on the instance's models | None                                           | A monthly allowance                                                               |
| Media storage (fair use)                    | {{free-storage-en}} across the billing account | {{storage-per-account-en}} per connected account, {{storage-minimum-en}} at least |

A billing account may make as many workspaces as it likes: they share one ladder, one set of free accounts and one allowance of each meter, so another workspace adds nothing free. The free tier is one per person: a person pays for one billing account at most, and staff may merge billing accounts opened to multiply it, or require a payment method of one.

## Billing accounts [#accounts]

Every workspace belongs to one billing account, which pays for it.

- The person who creates a workspace pays for it: each workspace they create joins their billing account, made with their first.
- A workspace made before billing accounts is on its oldest owner's.
- A [partner account](/developers/embedding)'s home workspace and the workspaces it owns are on the partner's billing account, apart from its owners' own.

The workspaces of an account share one plan and count their channels together: a free account holds the free tier's {{free-accounts}} accounts across all its workspaces.

The plan is ordered, cancelled and withdrawn from on the account's billing workspace, its oldest (a partner's home workspace), which holds the subscription. The Billing page of every other workspace of the account names it, with a link for its members, and orders nothing (409 `billed_elsewhere`). Every other workspace of the account reads its plan, connected accounts and limits, but none of its money or contract. A workspace with a plan subscription of its own from before keeps it alone until it ends, then joins its payer's account. On a server that does not bill, the accounts are kept and limit nothing.

### Hand a workspace to another owner [#transfer]

The payer of a workspace hands it to another of its owners on its Billing page. The offer is open for 7 days, and the owner it is made to accepts it there, in person, or declines it. Accepting moves the workspace to their billing account, made if they have none.

It is refused:

- when their account's plan has no room for the workspace's channels;
- for the billing workspace while its subscription may still charge: cancel it first, or hand over another workspace.

The API's `POST …/billing/transfer`, `POST …/billing/transfer/acceptance` and `DELETE …/billing/transfer` answer only a person in their own session.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="When the payer leaves">
    On a server that bills, the payer stays one of the workspace's owners
    until they hand it over: leaving it, being removed from it or losing the
    owner role answers 400 `PAYER_MUST_STAY`, and a reduction that would
    remove them `payerRequired`.

    On a server that does not bill nothing is refused: a workspace whose payer
    is no longer one of its owners moves to its oldest owner's account.

    When the payer closes their account, each workspace they paid for that
    others own goes to its oldest other owner's account, over its plan if that
    account has no room for its channels.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## What a plan limits [#limits]

A workspace never goes above its plan: each limit is checked when something is added, requests sent together included, and what the plan has no room for is refused.

| Limit             | How it is checked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Channels          | On the free tier, connecting an account past the first {{free-accounts}} of the billing account is refused, and with a payment method nothing is. An Instagram account is one channel whichever way it is connected: moving it from a Facebook Page to Instagram, or back, adds none, even on a full plan.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Members           | Pending invitations included, when inviting and again when an invitation is accepted, on the plans priced by channels that limit them only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Posts             | Posts going out in a calendar month (UTC), on the plans priced by channels that limit them only. Checked again when each post is due.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Writing assistant | On the free tier and per-account pricing, a [usage meter](#meters): each request and each image counts once against the billing account's month, none without a payment method. On a plan priced by channels, requests per UTC day, as the plan gives them, an image of the instance's model costing 5 of them ([Generate an image](/guides/media#generate)). An image from the workspace's own [image provider](/guides/workspace#image-provider), and a suggestion from its own [text provider](/guides/workspace#text-provider), cost none, on any plan, and are never metered. |
| Media             | The bytes of images and videos stored, uploads in progress included. An upload the plan has no room for is refused before anything is sent; delete media to make room. The room an upload is given is held for it for six hours: finished later, it needs room again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

In the web app, a refusal opens a dialog that says which limit was reached, with what the workspace holds and its plan. It offers per-account pricing, with what the next account costs.

- Owners and admins add a payment method there, by card, as on the Billing page ([Ordering](#ordering)), and come back to what they were doing.
- Other members read who can.

Over the API, each refusal answers 402 `PlanLimitReached`, with the `limit`, what the plan `allowed`, what the workspace holds (`used`), its `plan`, the plan with room (`planWithRoom`: `payAsYouGo` when a payment method gives room, null when no plan holds that much) and the billing page (`billingUrl`). The writing assistant's daily allowance answers 429 `AssistantQuotaExceeded` with the same plan fields. The command line and MCP tools say the same in words, with the plan to order and the billing page.

## A workspace over its plan [#over]

A plan can shrink under a workspace: its trial ends, or its subscription is cancelled or left unpaid, and it is back on the free tier. Nothing is deleted, so the workspace may then hold more than its plan allows. With more channels than the plan allows across its billing account (or more members, on a plan priced by channels that limits them), it must come back within its plan before it does anything else.

- Every member who opens the workspace sees what it holds beyond the plan.
- Owners and admins can add a payment method, which holds everything, or choose which channels stay connected (and, on such a plan, which members stay). The others are disconnected or removed in one confirmed step, and pending invitations are revoked. Disconnecting is the same as disconnecting each channel by hand: its tokens are deleted and the platforms that allow it are asked to forget Peal. Other members read who can do this.
- More media than the plan allows refuses uploads only: the library stays open, to delete from.

<Callout
  type="warning"
  title="Nothing is published until the workspace is within its plan"
>
  Until then nothing is scheduled or published. Posts already scheduled are kept
  as they are and fail on their channels when due, with `planLimit` as the
  reason and nothing sent. Once the workspace is within its plan, post each
  again from its page.
</Callout>

Those posts are not moved back to drafts: a failed post keeps its time, text and channels, tells its author by notice and email, and is posted again in one step, where a draft would lose its schedule without telling anyone.

<Callout type="info" title="When Peal Cloud starts billing">
  A workspace on the free tier with more accounts than it includes keeps
  scheduling and publishing on the accounts it had then until a day Peal
  announces, and connects no more meanwhile. An account connected since is not
  covered, and a workspace whose subscription ends meanwhile keeps the accounts
  it had then, until that day. Its billing managers are told, in the app and by
  email, and every member sees the day.
</Callout>

Three days before a trial ends, the reminder lists what the workspace holds beyond the free tier. `GET /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/billing/standing` answers the same for every member: what is over, whether the workspace must come back within its plan, until when a grace keeps it publishing (`graceUntil`), and the plan that holds everything.

## Usage [#usage]

Under the plan, the workspace's **Billing** page shows what it did day by day over the last 7, 30 or 90 days: the posts that went out and the channels it had connected, as a chart and as a table, with the period's totals.

`GET /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/usage?from=2026-10-01&to=2026-10-31`, [`peal usage`](/developers/cli/workspace#usage) and the MCP tool [`get_usage`](/developers/mcp) answer the same to whoever reads the workspace's billing: owners and admins, an API key or an application given it, and a partner's keys in the workspaces the partner owns.

They are counts only: nothing is priced, charged or limited by them. A server that does not bill keeps and answers the same counts, with `plan` and `allowances` null.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="How usage is counted">
    - Days are UTC days, as every limit is. `from` and `to` are both included, 92 days at most; without them, the 30 days that end today. A range that ends before it starts, or is longer, answers 400 `invalid_date_range`.
    - Each day counts the posts that went out on it, one for each channel a post went out on, by platform. A thread counts once. A post sent again after it failed counts again, as it is another post on the platform. A post whose outcome could not be confirmed is not counted, even once someone confirms that it went out. The day also gives the workspace's requests to the writing assistant, which the note below copies: `null` for a day without the note, today's included, until it is taken.
    - Shortly after each UTC midnight (at 00:11, or up to 12 hours later after an outage), the server notes what every workspace holds then, for the day that just ended: the channels it connected itself, its members and the bytes of media it stores. A day without that note (before usage was counted, or a day the note could not be taken) answers `null`, and shows a dash on the page, never another day's figure.
    - The totals add up the period's posts, by platform, and its channel-days: each day's channels added up, the days without a note left out, with how many days they cover (`daysCounted`). For each calendar month the period touches, they also give the posts on platforms that bill the server for each post (X), with how many held a link. These count each part of a thread, as X bills each: a thread of three parts is one of the day's posts, and three here.
    - `current` is what each limit counts now, as on the Billing page. A workspace a partner owns counts its channels against the partner's plan: `sharedWith` names the partner's workspace.
    - The counts are kept {{keep-usage-en}}, then deleted; a range that starts earlier answers what is kept. They are deleted with the workspace, and its data export holds them.
    - A partner account reads the same across every workspace it holds, per customer: [Usage per customer](/developers/embedding/operate#usage).

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Ordering [#ordering]

Owners and admins order per-account pricing on the billing workspace's **Billing** page, in their own session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tick the box">
    Before anything is paid, they tick a box that is never ticked for them: "I
    ask Peal to start per-account pricing now: each connected account past the
    free ones, counted by the day, and each use past its monthly allowance are
    billed at the rate card’s prices, excluding tax, each month for the month
    past. I know that, as a consumer, I may withdraw within 14 days, with
    “Withdraw from contract here”, at the foot of every page and on this one,
    and then pay only for what was used." Peal records it with the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay at Stripe">
    Peal then opens Stripe's checkout from the **Order with obligation to pay**
    button. Stripe asks for a card, though nothing is due yet, the billing
    address and, for a business, the VAT number, and computes the VAT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the confirmation">
    A confirmation comes by email, with the rates and the contract number, which
    cancels or withdraws without signing in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The subscription bills calendar months (UTC), from the first of the next month.
- Stripe's portal changes the payment method and gives the invoices and receipts, also once the plan has ended (**Invoices and receipts**).
- Only per-account pricing is ordered, monthly: another plan or a year is refused (409 `PlanNotSold`).
- An account that already has it, even past due or unpaid, orders nothing more (409 `AlreadySubscribed`): a failed payment is settled by updating the payment method in Stripe's portal.
- An order from a page whose box shows older words, or that names none, is refused (409 `ConsentChanged`): load the page again.
- A workspace on a plan priced by channels, or in a trial, may order it too: that plan or trial then ends with its period, or at once for a trial.

<Callout type="warning" title="No credit for a plan priced by channels">
  For now no credit is given for the plan's unused time: it stays paid until its
  period ends (up to a year for a yearly plan) while the accounts are billed per
  account from the move, so moving near the end of the period costs least.
</Callout>

## Cancelling [#cancelling]

Cancel online at any time with **Cancel your contract**: the link is at the foot of every page, signed in or not, and on the **Billing** page. The page asks for what identifies you and the contract, and nothing else: your name, the email address the acknowledgement goes to, the contract, and, if you wish, the day you want it to end. It then shows a summary to check, and **Notify the cancellation** sends it.

| How you cancel                                                                                              | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Signed in, as an owner or an admin                                                                          | Your name and address are filled in, you pick the contract from your workspaces, and the cancellation is applied at once. The **Billing** page has the same two steps for its own workspace.                                                                                                                                                           |
| With the contract number, which is in the email that confirmed the subscription and in the renewal reminder | Applied at once, without signing in, whatever address you give.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| With neither                                                                                                | Peal emails the address an acknowledgement. If that address manages a workspace's billing, the email holds a link, good for 30 days, that shows its contracts and applies the cancellation to the one you choose; otherwise a person looks at the request and writes to you. This keeps someone who only knows your address from ending your contract. |

Whichever way, the cancellation counts from the moment Peal received it, which the acknowledgement states: a renewal charged after that moment is refunded.

- Per-account pricing ends with the month, whose use the last invoice bills.
- A plan priced by channels ends with the period paid for, and nothing more is charged.
- With a later day, it ends on that day and the last period is charged only up to it.
- A trial ends at once.

The members who manage billing get the confirmation by email, with the date it takes effect. Deleting the workspace cancels at once too ([Workspaces and your account](/guides/workspace#delete-workspace)).

## Withdrawing within 14 days [#withdrawal]

As a consumer, you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of subscribing, without giving a reason, with **Withdraw from contract here**: at the foot of every page, signed in or not, and on the **Billing** page while the 14 days run, even after cancelling.

It works as cancelling does: your name, the address for the acknowledgement and the contract, a summary, then **Confirm withdrawal**. It is applied at once when you are signed in or give the contract number, and otherwise from the link Peal emails you. The 14 days and the refund count from the moment Peal received your statement, which the acknowledgement states with what you sent.

Withdrawing ends the subscription at once and refunds, for each payment since you subscribed, the part of the period it paid for not used, to the second, to your payment method within 14 days, through a credit note on each invoice. Per-account pricing is billed for the month past: withdrawing invoices its use to that moment, and nothing paid ahead is refunded.

<Callout type="info">
  A business that gave a VAT number Stripe verified has no right to withdraw,
  and can cancel instead.
</Callout>

## Trials [#trial]

No trial is offered any more: the free accounts replace it. A trial started before runs to its end, without a charge, and the workspace is then on the free tier, with its content. Three days before, the members who manage billing are reminded, with what the workspace holds beyond the free tier.

## Yearly plans [#renewal]

Yearly plans are no longer sold. A yearly plan priced by channels renews each year until cancelled. 45 days before it renews, and never less than a month before, the members who manage billing are reminded, in the app and by an email of its own, that the contract need not be renewed. The reminder gives:

- what Stripe will charge, VAT included;
- the last day to refuse the renewal, in a box;
- the contract number;
- how to cancel.

A price change is announced by email 30 days ahead, and applies from the next renewal.
