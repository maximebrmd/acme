Peal can connect a workspace to its ad accounts on Meta, show what their ads did beside what your posts did on their own, and boost a post you published through Peal, as an ad on Facebook or Instagram.

<Callout type="note" title="New">
  This feature is new: its API may still grow. It is off unless the operator of
  the instance turned it on, and an instance may offer reading without spending.
  Nothing spends until an owner allows an ad account to, with a monthly ceiling.
</Callout>

## What works today [#what-works]

- **Connect Meta ad accounts**, through a sign-in of its own, apart from the one that connects Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts as [channels](/channels).
- **Read campaigns and daily numbers**: each connected ad account's campaigns, ad sets and ads, those made outside Peal included, and what each ad did each day.
- **Organic and paid, side by side**: a published post's own counts on each channel, beside what the ads that promote it did.
- **Boost a published post**, on a Facebook Page, or on an Instagram account connected through its Page, with a budget, an end date and an audience.
- **Pause, resume and stop** a boost.
- **Notifications** to the workspace's owners about every boost, [webhook events](#webhooks) for your own systems, and an [audit trail](#audit) of every decision about money.
- The same operations through the [API](#api) and the SDKs, the [command line and MCP tools](#clients).

[What is not built](#not-built) lists the rest.

## Who can do what [#access]

Ads have four permissions. A member holds those of their [role](/scheduling#roles):

| Permission      | What it allows                                                                                                   | Who holds it               |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| `ads:read`      | See the ad accounts, their campaigns and numbers, a post's paid results, and the boosts                          | Owners, admins and editors |
| `ads:manage`    | Sign in to an ad network, connect and disconnect ad accounts, and pause or stop a boost                          | Owners and admins          |
| `ads:spend`     | Boost a post, resume a paused boost, and pause or stop one                                                       | Owners and admins          |
| `ads:authorize` | Decide whether an ad account may spend through Peal, set its monthly ceiling, and read the [audit trail](#audit) | Owners only                |

Some of it is done by a person only, signed in to Peal in their own session, never by an API key, an application or staff acting as a member:

- signing in to the ad network and choosing the ad accounts to connect (`ads:manage`), and only who signed in picks accounts from their sign-in;
- allowing an ad account to spend, and setting or changing its ceiling (`ads:authorize`).

An [API key](/settings#api-keys) does what the ads permissions it was given allow, `ads:authorize` never among them. An OAuth application needs the scope `workspace:ads` too, which opens nothing alone: beside `workspace:write` for what `ads:manage` allows an application, beside `workspace:publish` for `ads:spend` ([What each permission allows](/agents#scopes)). Staff acting as a member only read.

## Connect ad accounts [#connect]

In the web app, open a workspace, then **Ads**.

1. An owner or an admin chooses to connect Meta, and signs in on Meta's page. Peal asks to read ads; on an instance that offers spending, it also asks for what making and running ads takes, which the person may decline: the accounts are then read, and cannot boost.
2. Back in Peal, the sign-in lists the ad accounts it reaches, each with its currency, time zone, status and spend cap. Nothing is connected yet.
3. They tick the accounts the workspace uses. Up to 25 are connected at once.

A sign-in is completed once, within 15 minutes, by who started it. One that connected no account is removed after a day. Peal stores the sign-in's access token encrypted, and never shows it.

Spending is off on every newly connected account. An account whose time zone Peal does not know cannot be connected.

When the network refuses a sign-in's token, its accounts show that they need reconnecting, the members who manage ad accounts are [told](#notices), and nothing is read of those accounts, nor boosted there, until the person who signed in does it again.

Disconnecting an ad account removes what Peal read of it; removing a sign-in removes its token and every account it connects. Their boosts' records stay with the workspace, and what they committed in a month still counts against the ceiling when the same account is connected again. Both are refused while a boost of theirs runs, may run, is paused or is being made (`AdAccountBusy`), as a paused campaign can run again: stop it first. What runs at the network that Peal did not make is left as it is.

## What Peal reads [#reading]

Every hour, Peal reads each connected ad account from its network: its campaigns, ad sets and ads, and each ad's numbers for each day of the account's calendar.

| Number      | What it is                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Spend       | What the ad cost, in the account's [minor units](#money)                                                |
| Impressions | How often it was shown                                                                                  |
| Reach       | How many people saw it that day. Summed over several days, someone seen on several days counts each day |
| Clicks      | Clicks on the ad                                                                                        |
| Results     | Reactions, comments and shares the network counts on the post. Missing, not zero, when it reported none |

- The first read of an account takes the last 90 days. Later ones read the last 7 days again, as networks correct recent days for a while.
- A read stops once the account reaches 75% of the rate limit its network gives it, and goes on at a later hour: Peal never uses an ad account's whole allowance.
- A day of numbers is kept 396 days (13 months), so a month can be compared with the same one a year before.
- Numbers are the network's own, read with a delay of up to an hour or more. They are not an invoice: what the network bills is in the ad account.

Peal keeps counts and money. It never reads who saw an ad.

### Organic and paid for a post [#post-performance]

A published post's page shows, side by side, its organic counts on each channel, as [Analytics](/analytics) last read them, and what the ads that promote it did, by ad account, with its boosts. An ad made outside Peal that promotes the same post is counted too.

The two are never added together: what a platform counts on a post already includes what its ads brought. Amounts in different currencies are never added either, so paid totals come one per ad account. The organic side is empty for someone who may not read analytics, and for a platform whose counts Peal [does not read](/analytics#schedule), such as Facebook Pages.

## Money [#money]

Every amount is an integer in the **minor units** of the ad account's currency, as the network counts them: cents for `EUR` and `USD`, whole yen for `JPY`. An ad account's `minorUnitDigits`, 2 or 0, says how to show one: divide by 10 to that power, with the `currency` (an ISO 4217 code). `5000` is 50.00 € on a `EUR` account, and 5,000 ¥ on a `JPY` one.

Do the arithmetic on the integers, and never on decimals. A boost's budget is in its ad account's currency, and one in another currency is refused.

The ad network bills the advertiser directly, on the ad account's payment method. Peal never handles the money, and takes no payment details for ads.

### What a boost commits [#commitment]

Before a boost is accepted, Peal counts the most it can cost, its **commitment**:

- a `total` budget commits its amount;
- a `daily` budget commits, for each calendar week of the account's time zone (Sunday to Saturday) the run touches, its amount and 75% more for each day of the run in that week, and never more than 7 times its amount in a week. Meta's page "About daily budgets" says why: on any day the network may spend up to 75% over a daily budget, 175% of it, and in a week ending on Saturday at midnight never more than 7 times it.

A daily budget of 10.00 € over a run of two days commits 35.00 €. Over a whole week, Sunday to Saturday, it commits 70.00 €.

### The monthly ceiling [#ceiling]

An owner allows an ad account to spend through Peal, with a **monthly ceiling** in the account's currency. The ceiling is the most the boosts made through Peal may commit on that account in one calendar month of its time zone.

- A boost that would pass it is refused before anything is reserved or sent (`SpendCeilingExceeded`), in the fullest month its run touches.
- It is **not a cap on the ad account itself**: ads made elsewhere, at the network or with another tool, are not counted, and not held back.
- A ceiling may always be lowered, under running boosts too: they run on, and nothing new starts or resumes until there is room.
- Turning spending off is refused while a boost runs or may run: stop it first.
- Peal never turns spending on, raises a ceiling or raises a budget on its own.

A stopped boost's commitment is counted down to what it can have cost: nothing if it never ran, the days it ran for a daily budget, its whole budget for a total one.

### The account's own spend cap [#spend-cap]

An ad account may have a spend cap of its own, set at the network, which covers everything the account spends. Peal reads it, with what the account spent against it, and refuses a boost the cap leaves too little room for (`AdAccountSpendCapExceeded`). The cap is raised at the network, never by Peal. To limit an ad account as a whole, set this cap: the monthly ceiling only limits Peal's own boosts.

## Boost a post [#boosts]

A boost promotes one post the workspace published through Peal, on one ad account, as an ad that aims for engagement. From the post's page, a member who may spend chooses the channel, the ad account and:

| What                  | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The post              | Published through Peal on a Facebook Page, or on an Instagram account connected through its Facebook Page. Not an Instagram account connected with Instagram Login. Not a [story or a reel](/scheduling#formats) |
| The budget            | `total` for the whole run, or `daily` for each day of it, above zero, in the ad account's currency                                                                                                               |
| The end               | Required. At least a day after the start (more than a day with a daily budget), 90 days at most                                                                                                                  |
| The start             | Now, or up to 30 days ahead                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| The audience          | 1 to 25 countries, and an age range from 18 to 65, where 65 means "65 and over". Nothing finer                                                                                                                   |
| The category          | The advertiser declares the ad is of no special category. An ad about housing, employment, financial products and services, or social issues, elections or politics is refused                                   |
| Beneficiary and payer | Who benefits from the ad and who pays for it, 512 characters each: required when any country of the audience is in the European Union                                                                            |

Before the last step, Peal shows a quote: what the boost commits, the days it touches, the ceiling, what the account's other boosts already commit, and the room the account's own cap leaves. A quote checks everything and reserves nothing.

Once confirmed, the boost is accepted as `requested`, its commitment counted against the ceiling, and Peal makes it in the background: the campaign, its ad set and its ad are created paused, then turned on, the campaign last. The post's page, and the [notifications](#notices), say how that ended. The ad network may still review the ad before it shows it, refuse it or stop it, by its own rules.

### Pause, resume and stop [#control]

- **Pause** tells the network to pause the boost's campaign; the boost is `paused` once the network confirms. Its budget stays counted.
- **Resume** runs a paused boost again until its end. It spends, so it is checked as a new boost is: spending still allowed, the account usable, the ceiling as it now is.
- **Stop** ends a boost for good; one not yet made is never made. A stopped boost does not run again.

Pausing and stopping are always available, to those who may spend and to those who manage ad accounts, and asked twice they are done once. They take effect when the network applies them. When the network does not answer, the boost stands where it did (`AdNetworkUnavailable`), and asking again later is safe. A boost can always be paused or deleted at the network itself.

### States [#states]

| State         | What it means                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `requested`   | Accepted, and being made. Paused at the network, spending nothing                                                                                     |
| `active`      | Running                                                                                                                                               |
| `paused`      | Paused by a member                                                                                                                                    |
| `ended`       | Its end date passed                                                                                                                                   |
| `stopped`     | Stopped by a member, for good                                                                                                                         |
| `failed`      | It could not be made, and nothing ran. `failureReason` says why                                                                                       |
| `unconfirmed` | Peal told the network to run it and could not read back whether it does. **It may be spending.** Its budget stays counted, and stopping it settles it |

An unconfirmed boost is never sent again by Peal on its own. Someone checks it, on the post's page or in the ad account, and stops it if it must not run.

### Why a boost failed [#failure-reasons]

A failed boost spent nothing.

| `failureReason`       | What happened                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `accountUnusable`     | The ad account cannot run ads now.                                                             |
| `accountSpendCap`     | The account's own spend cap leaves too little room.                                            |
| `ceilingExceeded`     | The monthly ceiling was lowered meanwhile, and leaves too little room.                         |
| `spendingOff`         | Spending was turned off for the ad account meanwhile.                                          |
| `currencyMismatch`    | The budget's currency is not the ad account's.                                                 |
| `budgetTooLow`        | The budget is under the network's minimum.                                                     |
| `postNotPromotable`   | The post was deleted, or cannot be shown as an ad.                                             |
| `permissionMissing`   | The sign-in lacks a permission ads need.                                                       |
| `reconnectNeeded`     | The network refused the sign-in, which its person renews by signing in again.                  |
| `networkUnavailable`  | The network kept refusing for now. Peal tries a few times over about two hours, then gives up. |
| `refused`             | The network refused for a reason of its own, which `networkCode` carries.                      |
| `spendingUnavailable` | The instance no longer offers spending.                                                        |

## Safety rules [#safety]

Everything that spends follows the same rules, whatever asks for it:

- **Off by default, twice.** Ads are off unless the instance's operator turns them on, and spending is off on each ad account until an owner turns it on, with an explicit monthly ceiling.
- **Only an owner decides what may be spent**, in person. Peal never turns spending on, raises a ceiling or raises a budget on its own.
- **Checked before anything is reserved or sent**: the boost's own rules, the ceiling, the account's spend cap, the account's status and its sign-in.
- **An end date is required.** No boost runs without one, nor for more than 90 days.
- **Created paused, turned on last.** Nothing can spend before the whole boost exists as it was asked.
- **Never sent twice blindly.** A request whose answer was lost is looked up at the network before anything is sent again. When Peal still cannot tell, the boost is `unconfirmed`, and a person decides.
- **Pause and stop are always there**, for those who spend and those who manage ad accounts.
- **Every decision is recorded** in the [audit trail](#audit), with who made it and the amounts.

## Notifications [#notices]

The workspace's owners hear about each boost, in the app and by email: it started, it ended, it used its whole budget, it could not be made, or Peal could not confirm that it runs. The members who manage ad accounts hear when an ad network sign-in must be renewed. These emails are always sent: an owner answers for what is spent. [Notifications](/notifications#events) lists them with the others.

## The audit trail [#audit]

Every decision about spending and every change to a boost is recorded, with who made it and the amounts: an ad account connected or disconnected, spending turned on or off, a ceiling changed, and a boost requested, started, unconfirmed, failed, paused, resumed, stopped or ended. A line names a member, or an API key, and the application that acted for the member when one did; what Peal's own jobs found, such as an end, names nobody.

Owners read it, and nobody else (`ads:authorize`), newest first, 100 lines a page. A line is kept 396 days (13 months). Once a member closes their account, their lines stay without their name.

## Through the API [#api]

The `ads` group is under `/api/workspaces/{workspaceId}/ads`. Paths below start there.

| Operation               | Request                                     | What it does                                                              | Needs                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `ads.status`            | `GET /`                                     | Says what the instance offers: `off`, `read` or `spend`, and the networks | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.listConnections`   | `GET /connections`                          | Lists the sign-ins to ad networks, never a token                          | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.connect`           | `POST /connections/{network}`               | Starts a sign-in, and answers the page to send the browser to             | `ads:manage`, in person     |
| `ads.completeConnect`   | `POST /connections/{network}/callback`      | Completes it, and answers the ad accounts it reaches                      | `ads:manage`, in person     |
| `ads.reachableAccounts` | `GET /connections/{connectionId}/accounts`  | Lists the ad accounts a sign-in reaches now                               | `ads:manage`, who signed in |
| `ads.connectAccounts`   | `POST /connections/{connectionId}/accounts` | Connects some of them, by `externalIds`                                   | `ads:manage`, who signed in |
| `ads.disconnect`        | `DELETE /connections/{connectionId}`        | Removes a sign-in and the accounts it connects                            | `ads:manage`                |
| `ads.listAccounts`      | `GET /accounts`                             | Lists the connected ad accounts                                           | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.getAccount`        | `GET /accounts/{accountId}`                 | Shows one, with what its boosts commit this month                         | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.removeAccount`     | `DELETE /accounts/{accountId}`              | Disconnects one                                                           | `ads:manage`                |
| `ads.setSpending`       | `PUT /accounts/{accountId}/spending`        | Turns its spending on or off, with a monthly ceiling                      | `ads:authorize`, in person  |
| `ads.listCampaigns`     | `GET /accounts/{accountId}/campaigns`       | Lists its campaigns, with what their ads did over some days               | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.listAds`           | `GET /accounts/{accountId}/ads`             | Lists its ads, those of one campaign with `campaignId`                    | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.listInsights`      | `GET /accounts/{accountId}/insights`        | Sums what its ads did each day                                            | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.postPerformance`   | `GET /posts/{postId}`                       | Shows a post's organic and paid numbers, and its boosts                   | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.quoteBoost`        | `POST /boosts/quote`                        | Checks a boost and says what it would commit, reserving nothing           | `ads:spend`                 |
| `ads.createBoost`       | `POST /boosts`                              | **Spends money.** Boosts a published post. Answers 202                    | `ads:spend`                 |
| `ads.listBoosts`        | `GET /boosts`                               | Lists the boosts, newest first, 50 a page, those of a post with `postId`  | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.getBoost`          | `GET /boosts/{boostId}`                     | Shows a boost, with the journal of the requests sent for it               | `ads:read`                  |
| `ads.pauseBoost`        | `POST /boosts/{boostId}/pause`              | Pauses it                                                                 | `ads:spend` or `ads:manage` |
| `ads.resumeBoost`       | `POST /boosts/{boostId}/resume`             | **Spends money.** Runs a paused boost again                               | `ads:spend`                 |
| `ads.stopBoost`         | `POST /boosts/{boostId}/stop`               | Stops it for good                                                         | `ads:spend` or `ads:manage` |
| `ads.auditTrail`        | `GET /audit`                                | Lists the audit trail, 100 lines a page                                   | `ads:authorize`             |

The three reports take `from` and `to`, calendar days of the account such as `2026-11-01`, both included and at most 366 days apart: the last 30 days when left out. Campaigns and ads come a thousand at most. `ads.listBoosts` and `ads.auditTrail` answer a `nextCursor` to pass as `cursor` for the next page.

The SDKs have the same operations in their `ads` group.

### Quote, then boost [#api-boost]

Ask for a quote, show it to whoever decides, then send the same body to create the boost:

```bash
curl https://api.example.com/api/workspaces/$WORKSPACE_ID/ads/boosts/quote \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PEAL_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "accountId": "019a7902-1f4b-7a60-9c12-7d8e0f2a4b55",
    "postId": "019a6f3e-8c2a-7b1e-9f40-3d5a1c2b4e01",
    "channelId": "019a5d14-6a2c-7b85-8f3d-1e2c4a6b8d90",
    "budget": { "kind": "total", "amount": 5000 },
    "currency": "EUR",
    "endsAt": "2026-11-19T09:00:00.000Z",
    "audience": { "countries": ["FR", "BE"], "ageMin": 25, "ageMax": 54 },
    "specialAdCategory": "none",
    "beneficiary": "Example Bakery SAS",
    "payer": "Example Bakery SAS"
  }'
```

The quote answers `commitment`, the `days` the run touches, the `ceiling`, what the account's other boosts already commit in the fullest month the run touches (`committed`), `capRoom`, null when the account has no cap of its own, and `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `currency` and `minorUnitDigits`.

`POST …/ads/boosts` takes the same body, and an `Idempotency-Key` header, which is required: any text of your choosing, 255 characters at most, new for each boost. The same key with the same request answers the boost it made, and never makes another; with another request it answers 409 `BoostKeyReused`. A request whose answer was lost is safe to send again with its key.

It answers 202 with the boost, `requested`: read it again, or listen for its [events](#webhooks), to learn whether it runs. `ads.getBoost` answers its `steps`, the journal of what Peal sent the network: each step, when, and how it ended (`done`, `refused`, and `found`, `absent` or `unknown` when a lost answer was looked up).

### Errors [#errors]

Errors come in the API's format, each with a `code` in snake case beside its name ([Errors](/api-conventions#errors)).

| Status | Error                       | When                                                                                                                                                |
| ------ | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 404    | `AdsUnavailable`            | Ads are off on the instance, or the network asked for is not set up on it.                                                                          |
| 409    | `AdSpendingUnavailable`     | The instance offers ads for reading only. Nothing was spent or changed.                                                                             |
| 422    | `AdSignInFailed`            | A sign-in did not come to a connection. `reason`: `denied`, `expired`, `failed`, or `notConfigured` when the instance has no key to encrypt with.   |
| 404    | `AdConnectionNotFound`      | No such sign-in, or it is another member's.                                                                                                         |
| 404    | `AdAccountNotFound`         | No such ad account, or the sign-in does not reach the account asked for.                                                                            |
| 404    | `BoostNotFound`             | No such boost.                                                                                                                                      |
| 503    | `AdNetworkUnavailable`      | The network did not answer, asked to slow down, or could not confirm a change. Nothing is known to have changed; `retryAfterSeconds`, when it said. |
| 409    | `AdAccountBusy`             | `boosts` boosts of the account run, may run, are being made or, for a disconnection, are paused: stop them first.                                   |
| 409    | `AdAccountUnusable`         | The account cannot take a boost now. `reason`: `status`, `reconnectNeeded` or `permission`.                                                         |
| 409    | `AdSpendingNotEnabled`      | No owner allowed this ad account to spend through Peal.                                                                                             |
| 409    | `SpendCeilingExceeded`      | The boost would pass the monthly ceiling: `ceiling`, `committed`, `requested`, in minor units of `currency`.                                        |
| 409    | `AdAccountSpendCapExceeded` | The account's own cap leaves `room`, and the boost would commit `requested`.                                                                        |
| 422    | `CeilingCurrencyMismatch`   | The ceiling is not in the account's currency, which `accountCurrency` gives.                                                                        |
| 422    | `BoostInvalid`              | The boost breaks Peal's rules. `violations` lists every one, below.                                                                                 |
| 422    | `PostNotPromotable`         | The post cannot be boosted there. `reason`: `notPublished`, `platform`, or `connection` for an Instagram account connected with Instagram Login.    |
| 409    | `BoostStateConflict`        | The boost's `state` does not allow this: one that failed, ended or was stopped does not run again, and only a paused one resumes.                   |
| 409    | `BoostKeyReused`            | The `Idempotency-Key` already names a boost that asked for something else.                                                                          |

| `violations`          | What is wrong                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `currencyMismatch`    | The budget's currency is not the ad account's.                                      |
| `budgetEmpty`         | The budget is zero.                                                                 |
| `endsBeforeStart`     | The run ends before it starts, or has ended.                                        |
| `runTooShort`         | The run is shorter than a day, or, with a daily budget, not longer than one.        |
| `runTooLong`          | The run is longer than 90 days.                                                     |
| `startsTooFarAhead`   | The run starts more than 30 days ahead.                                             |
| `ageRange`            | The youngest age is above the oldest.                                               |
| `specialCategory`     | The ad is of a special category, which Peal does not boost.                         |
| `beneficiaryRequired` | The audience is in the European Union, and the beneficiary or the payer is missing. |

The [API reference](/reference) lists each endpoint's parameters and answers.

## Command line and MCP [#clients]

The [command line](/cli) has the ads commands under `peal ads`: run `peal ads --help` for the list. [AI assistants](/mcp#tools) have ads tools, which follow the same permissions and the same rules: a tool that spends says so in its description, and needs the application to hold `workspace:publish` and `workspace:ads`.

Neither signs in to an ad network, turns spending on or changes a ceiling: those are done in the web app, by a person.

## Your data [#data]

- Deleting a workspace deletes its sign-ins with their tokens, its ad accounts, what was read of them, its boosts, their journal and its audit trail. It is refused while a boost runs, may run or is being made: stop it first.
- Closing your account removes your id from what stays with the workspaces you shared: the sign-ins you made stay with their ad accounts, without it, until the account is disconnected or the workspace deleted.
- The [copy of your data](/settings#export) holds, for each workspace you are the only member of, an `ads` object: its sign-ins without their tokens, its ad accounts, its boosts and its audit trail.
- When someone asks Meta to delete what an instance holds about them, Peal removes the sign-ins they made and the ad accounts these connect; when they remove the app from their Meta account, their sign-ins are marked to renew.

What an ad needs goes to the ad network: the post's id, the budget, the dates, the audience, and the beneficiary and payer.

## What Meta asks of the operator [#operator]

Ads are off until the instance's operator sets [`PEAL_ADS`](/environment-variables#ads) to `read` or `spend`. A self-hosted instance brings its own Meta app with ads access: Peal Cloud's app does not serve it.

Ads use Meta's main app, the one Facebook Pages connect with (`META_APP_ID`, `META_APP_SECRET`, in [Self-hosting](/self-hosting#meta)), and [`PEAL_TOKEN_KEYS`](/environment-variables#channels) to encrypt the sign-ins' tokens. `PEAL_ADS` set to `read` or `spend` without that app stops the server at startup. Register on the app, as a valid OAuth redirect URI, the address [the command](/self-hosting#platform-addresses) prints for ads.

These are what Meta's documentation asks for at the time of writing: confirm each with Meta when you apply, as its rules change ([Authorization](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/get-started/authorization), [Rate limiting](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/overview/rate-limiting)).

- **Permissions.** `ads_read` to read ad accounts and their reports. To boost: `ads_management`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement` and `pages_manage_ads`.
- **App Review, with advanced access** for each permission, to act for other people's ad accounts. An app that only manages its own ad account can work with standard access.
- **Business verification** of the business that owns the app.
- **Marketing API access tier.** An app starts with limited access, whose rate limits per ad account are low, and gets full access after App Review and enough successful calls: Meta's page says 500 calls in 15 days, with an error rate under 15%.

Set `PEAL_ADS=read` until Meta granted the app `ads_management`: members connect ad accounts and read them, the sign-in asks for reading only, and everything that spends answers `AdSpendingUnavailable`. Move to `spend` afterwards; people who signed in before then sign in again to allow running ads.

The server calls Meta's Graph API for ads, as it does for Facebook Pages. Whatever the access tier, Peal stops reading an ad account at 75% of the rate limit Meta reports for it.

## What is not built [#not-built]

- Other ad networks than Meta's.
- Creating campaigns, ad sets or ads with creatives of their own: Peal boosts a post that is already published.
- Boosting a post that was not published through Peal, or one on an Instagram account connected with Instagram Login.
- Changing a running boost's budget or audience: stop it, and make another.
- Interests, custom audiences, gender, or locations finer than countries.
- Special ad categories: housing, employment, financial products and services, social issues, elections or politics.
- Conversions, lead forms and automated rules.
