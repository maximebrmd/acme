From a new account to your first scheduled post, in the web app. Each step links to the guide that says more.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workspace">
    Sign in, or choose **Create an account**, then **Create a workspace**. A
    workspace holds a team's channels, posts and members; you can invite people
    to it later ([Workspace settings](/guides/workspace)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    Open **Channels** and, under the platform, choose its **Connect** button,
    such as **Connect Bluesky**. Sign in on the platform and allow Peal: the
    account becomes a channel the workspace posts to. Each [platform's
    page](/platforms) says what it asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a post">
    Choose **New post**, tick the channels it goes to, and write its text. The
    composer counts each platform's limit and shows a preview per channel, and a
    channel can have its own text ([Write a post](/guides/posts)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Under **When**, pick the date, the time and the time zone, then choose
    **Schedule**, or **Publish now** to send it at once. The post shows on the
    **Calendar** ([Schedule a post](/guides/posts/schedule)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it">
    Open the post from the calendar once it went out: its page shows, for each
    channel, its status, the post's address on the platform, or why it failed
    ([Follow up on a post](/guides/posts/schedule#follow-up)). The bell tells
    you how it went, and email too if you choose
    ([Notifications](/guides/workspace/notifications)).
  </Step>
</Steps>
