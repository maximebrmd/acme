## Connect [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Facebook">
    Under **Facebook Pages**, choose **Connect Facebook Pages**, sign in with
    Facebook, and allow Peal to list your Pages and post on them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Pages">
    Back in Peal, tick the Pages the workspace posts to, then **Connect**: each
    becomes a channel. Choose within 15 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- A sign-in that reaches one Page connects it straight away.
- Only Pages you may create content on are listed. Those the workspace already has are marked: choosing one reconnects it.
- When connecting fails before any Page is connected, Peal says why, and you can choose again, or **Sign in with Facebook again** for a new list.
- Once the choice has expired or been made, only signing in again brings the Pages back.

## Options [#options]

Each option is sent only when you choose it. Some apply only to some media: the composer shows them for those, and scheduling refuses them on another post. In the composer, a video has its **Video title**. **API name** is the option's name through the API.

| Option          | API name     | Values                                                                        | Left out    |
| --------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Format**      | `format`     | `story` or `reel` (see [stories and reels](/guides/posts#formats)), or `feed` | A feed post |
| **Video title** | `videoTitle` | A video or a reel: one line, 255 characters at most                           | No title    |
