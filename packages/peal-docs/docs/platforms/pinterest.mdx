## Connect [#connect]

Under **Pinterest**, choose **Connect Pinterest**, sign in on Pinterest, and allow Peal to:

- see your boards and Pins;
- create Pins;
- read your account's name and picture.

Peal asks for nothing about secret boards, so it never lists them and cannot pin to them.

**Channels** offers Pinterest once the Peal server is set up for it (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

## Options [#options]

The composer offers these options. **API name** is the option's name through the API.

| Option                     | API name         | In the composer                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Board**                  | `boardId`        | Required, one of the account's                                        |
| **Board section**          | `boardSectionId` | A section of the chosen board                                         |
| **Title**                  | `title`          | 100 characters at most                                                |
| **Link**                   | `link`           | Where a click on the Pin leads, starting with `http://` or `https://` |
| **Video cover, at second** | `coverSecond`    | The first frame unless you choose                                     |
| **Dominant colour**        | `dominantColor`  | Such as `#6E7874`                                                     |

### Boards [#boards]

- On a Pinterest channel, the composer reads the account's boards on Pinterest, up to 500 of them, and, once you chose one, its sections.
- A board deleted since, or past those read, stays chosen and shows as not in the list.
- When Pinterest does not answer, **Try again** reads the list again.
- Peal creates no board: make one on Pinterest first.

A Pin has one alt text, the first file's, and a carousel's images share the Pin's title, description and link.

## Comments [#comments]

Peal does not read comments on Pinterest yet.

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers Pinterest once its app is registered: see [Pinterest](/self-hosting/platforms#pinterest). Until then, **Channels** does not offer it.
