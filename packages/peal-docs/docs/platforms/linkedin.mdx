## Connect [#connect]

Under **LinkedIn**, the button is **Connect LinkedIn**. On a server set up for company pages, there are two buttons instead: **Connect your profile** and **Connect company pages**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Your profile">
    Choose **Connect LinkedIn** (or **Connect your profile**), sign in on
    LinkedIn and allow Peal. Peal connects your own LinkedIn profile, and posts
    as you. It asks LinkedIn for your name and picture, and to publish posts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Company pages">
    **Connect company pages** signs you in to a second LinkedIn app, then lists
    the pages you may post to, where you are an administrator or a content
    administrator. Choose one or several: each becomes a channel that posts as
    the page.

    Peal asks LinkedIn to post as your pages, to read their posts, and to list
    the pages you administer and read their statistics; nothing about your own
    profile. A page keeps working while you keep your role on it: when you lose
    it, or leave the company, reconnect the page with another administrator's
    sign-in.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Channels** offers LinkedIn, and company pages, once the Peal server is set up for each (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

### Stay connected [#expiry]

- LinkedIn lets Peal post for 60 days, then asks you to sign in again. The channel shows the date, and its workspace's owners and admins are told a week before.
- Reconnect before then: when you are signed in on LinkedIn, it does not ask again.
- A page's access lasts 60 days too, unless LinkedIn gave the server's app refresh tokens, which it reserves for approved partners. Peal then renews it a week ahead, for a year after the sign-in.

## Options [#options]

The composer offers these options. **API name** is the option's name through the API.

| Option                                   | API name          | What it takes                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Who can see this post**                | `visibility`      | Anyone unless you choose otherwise (`public`); connections only (`connections`) is for a profile; signed-in LinkedIn members (`loggedIn`)                                                 |
| **Keep others from resharing this post** | `disableReshare`  | Yes or no                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Pages to mention**                     | `mentions`        | Write `@` and the page's full name in the text, as LinkedIn shows it, and list each page here as `Name <urn:li:organization:number>`, separated by semicolons. People cannot be mentioned |
| **Link to show as a card**               | `linkUrl`         | A link                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Link title**                           | `linkTitle`       | Required with a link                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Link description**                     | `linkDescription` | With a link                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Document title**                       | `documentTitle`   | For a PDF                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Comments [#comments]

Peal reads the comments under the posts of LinkedIn company pages, and you answer them from Peal: see [Inbox](/guides/engage/inbox#linkedin).

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers LinkedIn once its app is registered, and company pages once their app is too: see [LinkedIn](/self-hosting/platforms#linkedin) and [LinkedIn company pages](/self-hosting/platforms#linkedin-pages). Until then, **Channels** does not offer them.
