## Connect [#connect]

Under **Bluesky**, give your handle, such as `you.bsky.social`, to go straight to your server's sign-in page, or leave it empty to pick the account on Bluesky. Then choose **Connect Bluesky**.

**Channels** offers Bluesky once the Peal server is set up for it (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

## Options [#options]

No option is required, and each is sent only when you choose it. **API name** is the option's name through the API. **Parts** says what it applies to in a [thread](/guides/posts#threads).

| Option                                                                          | API name                                             | Values                                                                                                             | Left out   | Parts                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Languages**                                                                   | `languages`                                          | Up to three language tags, such as `en` or `pt-BR`                                                                 | None named | Every part                        |
| **Content label**                                                               | `contentLabel`                                       | `sexual`, `nudity`, `porn`, `graphic-media`                                                                        | No label   | Every part with images or a video |
| **Who can reply**                                                               | `replies`                                            | `everyone`, `nobody`, or `limited` to those of `replyFollowers`, `replyFollowing` and `replyMentioned` that are on | Everyone   | The thread                        |
| **Your followers**, **Accounts you follow**, **Accounts mentioned in the post** | `replyFollowers`, `replyFollowing`, `replyMentioned` | Yes or no, each; with `replies` limited, one at least                                                              | No         | The thread                        |
| **Keep others from quoting this post**                                          | `disableQuotes`                                      | Yes or no                                                                                                          | No         | Every part                        |

- A label describes the images or video: a part without media is sent without it.
- Who can reply and quoting are written with the post, in one request, so a post never goes out without them.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="A channel connected before Peal asked for reply and quote settings">
    Who can reply and quoting need the account to have been connected since Peal
    asks for them. On a channel connected before, scheduling refuses them, and
    its [capabilities](/developers#capabilities) say so
    (`optionNeedsReconnect`). In the composer, such a channel offers neither and
    says so. Reconnect the account on the **Channels** page to use them; nothing
    else changes.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="The account's server refuses the settings">
    When Peal could not read the channel's scopes and the account's server then
    refuses these settings, the post is not sent: the channel reads **Failed**,
    with the code `optionRefused`, and Peal does not send it again. Reconnect
    the account, or leave the settings out, and post it again.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Comments [#comments]

Peal reads the comments under your published posts there, and you answer them from Peal: see [Inbox](/guides/engage/inbox#other-platforms).

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers Bluesky once its app is registered: see [Bluesky](/self-hosting/platforms#bluesky). Until then, **Channels** does not offer it.
