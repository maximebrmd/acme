## Connect [#connect]

Peal posts to Instagram professional accounts, business or creator. It reaches them in one of two ways, which **Instagram** offers as two buttons when the server has both.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Instagram Login">
    **Connect with Instagram** is the default. Sign in to the professional
    account itself on Instagram, and allow Peal to read its profile, publish,
    and read its insights.

    - The account needs no Facebook Page.
    - Its access lasts 60 days, and Peal renews it on its own a week before it ends.
    - You may decline insights: the account then posts, without counts in **Analytics**.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Facebook Login">
    **Connect through a Facebook Page**: sign in with Facebook, as for Pages,
    then tick the Instagram accounts linked to your Pages.

    - An account posts with its Page's access, which does not expire, so it stays connected as long as you manage the Page.
    - Use it for an account run from a Page, or for what only Meta's Facebook Login API gives, such as tagging products, collaborators and hashtag search, which Peal does not use yet.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Both ways publish images, reels and carousels the same way, and read the same counts.

- The channels list says which way each Instagram channel came through, and reconnecting it signs in that way again.
- Connecting through Instagram Login an account the workspace has through a Page moves its channel there, with its posts and schedule: the workspace keeps one channel per account.

## Options [#options]

Each option is sent only when you choose it. Some apply only to some media: the composer shows them for those, and scheduling refuses them on another post. In the composer, these are a reel's settings and **Collaborators**. **API name** is the option's name through the API.

| Option                           | API name        | Values                                                              | Left out             |
| -------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Format**                       | `format`        | `story` (see [stories and reels](/guides/posts#formats)), or `feed` | A feed post          |
| **Also share to the feed**       | `shareToFeed`   | A reel: yes or no                                                   | As Instagram decides |
| **Cover image**                  | `coverMediaId`  | A reel: a JPEG image of the library, 8 MiB at most                  | The cover frame      |
| **Cover frame, in milliseconds** | `thumbOffsetMs` | A reel: its frame, in milliseconds from its start, within it        | The first frame      |
| **Audio name**                   | `audioName`     | A reel: one line, 100 characters at most                            | Instagram's name     |
| **Trial reel**                   | `trialReel`     | A reel: `manual` or `performance`                                   | Not a trial          |
| **Collaborators**                | `collaborators` | One to three Instagram usernames                                    | None                 |

- On Instagram, a reel is a feed post of one video. A story takes none of these options.
- A cover image wins over a cover frame: only the image is sent then.
- Instagram renames a reel's audio once.
- A trial reel is shown to accounts that do not follow yours first. It reaches your followers when you share it in the app (`manual`) or by itself when it does well (`performance`).
- Instagram shows the alt text of a post's images, up to 1,000 characters, and none on a video.

<Callout type="warning" title="Collaborators get an invitation">
  Each collaborator is another account, which receives an invitation to be named
  as a co-author: name only accounts you mean to invite. Instagram does not
  document collaborators for accounts connected with Instagram Login: when
  Instagram refuses such a post, the channel reads **Failed** with Meta's own
  code.
</Callout>
