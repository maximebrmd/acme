## Connect [#connect]

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Under **Google Business Profile**, choose **Connect Google Business
    Profile**, sign in with Google, and allow Peal to manage your Business
    Profile. Google has one permission for everything about a profile, and Peal
    uses it to list your locations and to make their posts, nothing else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the locations">
    A sign-in that reaches one location connects it. One that reaches several
    lists them, each with its address, for you to choose those this workspace
    posts to. Each location is a channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- Only locations Google has verified are listed: Google takes no post for the others. Verify the business in your Business Profile first, then connect again.
- One sign-in offers 200 locations at most.

<Callout type="warning" title="When a location changes hands">
  A channel keeps the account its location was reached through. If the location
  moves to another account or location group, or the person who connected it no
  longer manages it, its posts fail: disconnect it, and connect the location
  again.
</Callout>

**Channels** offers Google Business Profile once the Peal server is set up for it (see [On your own server](#self-hosting)).

## Options [#options]

The composer offers these options. **API name** is the option's name through the API.

| Option                       | API name          | In the composer                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Post type**                | `postType`        | An update unless you choose an event or an offer         |
| **Title**                    | `title`           | 58 characters at most; required for an event or an offer |
| **Starts**                   | `startsAt`        | Required for an event or an offer                        |
| **Ends**                     | `endsAt`          | Required for an event or an offer                        |
| **Button**                   | `callToAction`    | On an update or an event                                 |
| **Button link**              | `callToActionUrl` | On an update or an event                                 |
| **Coupon code**              | `couponCode`      | On an offer                                              |
| **Link to redeem the offer** | `redeemUrl`       | On an offer                                              |
| **Terms and conditions**     | `terms`           | On an offer                                              |

How Google Business Profile treats them:

- The post's text is the post's description.
- An event's or an offer's dates are on the location's own clock, whatever your time zone, and the end comes after the start.
- Every button opens its link, which starts with `https://` or `http://`, except **Call now**, which rings the number on the profile and takes no link.
- An offer shows Google's own button.

## Comments [#comments]

Peal does not read comments on Google Business Profile yet.

## On your own server [#self-hosting]

A self-hosted server offers Google Business Profile once its app is registered, with a Google project Google gave access to its Business Profile APIs: see [Google Business Profile](/self-hosting/platforms#google-business). Until then, **Channels** does not offer it.
