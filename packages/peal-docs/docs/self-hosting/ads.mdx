Ads stay off on a server of your own until you turn them on. This page says how, and what Meta asks of whoever runs the server's app.

## Turn ads on [#ads]

Ads are off until the instance's operator sets [`PEAL_ADS`](/self-hosting/environment-variables#ads):

| `PEAL_ADS`     | What workspaces may do                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `off` or unset | Nothing: ads are off                                          |
| `read`         | Connect Meta ad accounts and read their campaigns and numbers |
| `spend`        | Also boost published posts, which spends their money at Meta  |

A self-hosted instance brings its own Meta app with ads access: Peal Cloud's app does not serve it. Ads use Meta's main app, the one [Facebook Pages](/self-hosting/platforms#meta) connect with (`META_APP_ID`, `META_APP_SECRET`), and [`PEAL_TOKEN_KEYS`](/self-hosting/environment-variables#channels) to encrypt the sign-ins' tokens. `PEAL_ADS` set to `read` or `spend` without that app stops the server at startup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the ads permissions from Meta">
    Apply for what [Meta asks of the operator](#operator) on the app in
    `META_APP_ID`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the redirect address">
    Register on the app, as a valid OAuth redirect URI, the address [the
    command](/self-hosting/platforms#platform-addresses) prints for ads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start on read">
    Set `PEAL_ADS=read` until Meta granted the app `ads_management`. Members
    connect ad accounts and read them, the sign-in asks for reading only, and
    everything that spends answers `AdSpendingUnavailable`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to spend">
    Once Meta granted `ads_management`, set `PEAL_ADS=spend`. People who signed
    in before then sign in again to allow running ads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The server calls Meta's Graph API for ads, as it does for Facebook Pages. Whatever the access tier, Peal stops reading an ad account at 75% of the rate limit Meta reports for it.

## What Meta asks of the operator [#operator]

These are what Meta's documentation asks for at the time of writing. Confirm each with Meta when you apply, as its rules change ([Authorization](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/get-started/authorization), [Rate limiting](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/overview/rate-limiting)).

| Requirement                      | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Permissions                      | `ads_read` to read ad accounts and their reports. To boost: `ads_management`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement` and `pages_manage_ads`.                                                                     |
| App Review, with advanced access | For each permission, to act for other people's ad accounts. An app that only manages its own ad account can work with standard access.                                                                               |
| Business verification            | Of the business that owns the app                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Marketing API access tier        | An app starts with limited access, whose rate limits per ad account are low, and gets full access after App Review and enough successful calls: Meta's page says 500 calls in 15 days, with an error rate under 15%. |
