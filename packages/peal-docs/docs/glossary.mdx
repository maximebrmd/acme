The words Peal uses in the app, the API and these docs. Each entry links to the page that says more.

## Workspaces and people [#people]

### Workspace [#workspace]

What a team shares: its channels, posts, media, members, API keys and webhooks. A person may belong to several. [Workspace settings](/guides/workspace).

### Member and role [#member]

A person in a workspace, with a role that says what they may do: owner, admin, editor, contributor or viewer. [Who can do what](/guides/posts#roles).

### Billing account [#billing-account]

Who pays for a workspace. The workspaces a person creates share their billing account. [Billing accounts](/guides/workspace/billing#accounts).

### Staff [#staff]

The people who run a Peal server, with a console of their own at `/admin`. [Staff console](/self-hosting/staff).

## Channels [#channels]

### Platform [#platform]

A social network Peal posts to, such as Bluesky or Instagram. [Platforms](/platforms).

### Channel [#channel]

One account on a platform, connected to a workspace, that its posts go to. [Connect channels](/guides/channels).

### Channel set [#channel-set]

A saved selection of channels to start a post with. [Channel sets](/guides/posts/organise#sets).

### Default options [#default-options]

The options a channel gives each new post, such as a visibility. [Default options for a channel](/guides/posts/organise#defaults).

## Posts [#posts]

### Post [#post]

One text, with its media and options, sent to one or more channels at one time. [Write a post](/guides/posts).

### Target [#target]

A post on one of its channels, with its own status, address and attempts. The API's `targets` list them.

### Draft [#draft]

A post with no time yet, which goes out nowhere. [Drafts](/guides/posts/drafts#drafts).

### Part and thread [#thread]

The parts after a post's first, sent as replies on the platforms that take threads. [Threads](/guides/posts#threads).

### Platform options [#options]

What a platform asks of a post beyond its text and media, such as a YouTube title. Each [platform's page](/platforms) lists its own.

### Format [#format]

The kind of post a channel receives on Instagram and Facebook: a feed post, a story or a reel. [Stories and reels](/guides/posts#formats).

### Queue [#queue]

A channel's time slots, which posts added to it fill in order. [Queues](/guides/posts/queues#queues).

### Feed [#feed]

A web feed (RSS, Atom or JSON Feed), such as a blog's, each new item of which becomes a post. [Post from a feed](/guides/posts/queues#feeds).

### Tag and signature [#tag]

A coloured label that files posts, and a text that ends them. [Tags, signatures and channel sets](/guides/posts/organise).

### Approval [#approval]

A post that waits for someone to approve it before it may go out. A **review link** shows it to people without a Peal account. [Review and approval](/guides/posts/review).

### Media library [#media-library]

A workspace's images and videos, ready to attach to posts, each with its alt text. [Images and videos](/guides/media).

## After publishing [#after]

### Inbox [#inbox]

The comments people leave under your published posts, which you answer from Peal. [Inbox](/guides/engage/inbox).

### Boost [#boost]

A published post promoted on Facebook or Instagram with a connected ad account's money. [Boost a post](/guides/engage/boosts).

## Building on Peal [#building]

### API key [#api-key]

A workspace's credential for programs, with the permissions it was given. It starts with `peal_`. [API keys](/guides/workspace#api-keys).

### Partner account [#partner]

An account that gives each of its own customers a workspace, through the API. [Embedding Peal](/developers/embedding).

### Connect link [#connect-link]

A hosted page where someone connects their accounts to a workspace without a Peal account. [Let someone else connect their account](/guides/channels#connect-links).

### Webhook endpoint [#webhook]

An address on your server that Peal sends events to, signed with its secret. Each send is a **delivery**. [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks).

### Idempotency key [#idempotency]

A key that makes a request safe to send again: within 24 hours, the same key and body answer what the first request made, and make nothing more. [Idempotency](/developers/conventions/idempotency).

### MCP [#mcp]

The protocol AI assistants use to call Peal's tools. [Connect an MCP client](/developers/mcp).
