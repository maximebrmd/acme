A channel is a social account connected to a workspace: a Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn or X account today. Posts go to channels, so connect at least one before you schedule anything.

Open a workspace from **Workspaces**, then **Channels**. Every member sees the workspace's channels; only owners and admins connect, reconnect and disconnect them.

<Callout type="note" title="Four platforms so far">
  Acme connects Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn and X accounts. The other platforms
  it knows, such as Threads, Instagram and Facebook, cannot be connected yet.
  **Channels** offers only the platforms the Acme server is set up for.
</Callout>

## Connect an account [#connect]

You sign in on the platform, agree to what Acme asks, and the platform sends you back to Acme, which finishes the connection. Finish it in the same browser tab you started it in, within 15 minutes.

Connecting an account the workspace already has reconnects its channel rather than adding a second one. Several workspaces may connect the same account.

### Mastodon [#mastodon]

Under **Mastodon**, give the account's server, such as `mastodon.social`, or the account's own address, such as `@you@mastodon.social`, then choose **Connect Mastodon**.

Acme works with any Mastodon server on the public internet, reached over HTTPS. The first time someone connects an account on a server, Acme registers itself there as an application. It asks to read the account's profile, and to publish posts and media.

### Bluesky [#bluesky]

Under **Bluesky**, give your handle, such as `you.bsky.social`, to go straight to your server's sign-in page, or leave it empty to pick the account on Bluesky. Then choose **Connect Bluesky**.

The Acme server must be set up for Bluesky: on a self-hosted instance, see [Bluesky](/self-hosting#bluesky). Until then, **Channels** does not offer it.

### LinkedIn [#linkedin]

Under **LinkedIn**, choose **Connect LinkedIn**, sign in on LinkedIn and allow Acme. Acme connects your own LinkedIn profile, and posts as you; company pages cannot be connected yet. It asks LinkedIn for your name and picture, and to publish posts.

LinkedIn lets Acme post for 60 days, then asks you to sign in again: the channel shows the date, and its workspace's owners and admins are told a week before. Reconnect before then: when you are signed in on LinkedIn, it does not ask again.

The Acme server must be set up for LinkedIn: on a self-hosted instance, see [LinkedIn](/self-hosting#linkedin). Until then, **Channels** does not offer it.

### X [#x]

Under **X**, choose **Connect X**, sign in on X and allow Acme. It asks to read and write posts, to upload media, and to stay connected, so it can renew its access without you.

The Acme server must be set up for X: on a self-hosted instance, see [X](/self-hosting#x). Until then, **Channels** does not offer it.

## Statuses [#statuses]

Each channel shows one of these:

| Status           | What it means                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Connected        | Acme can post to the account.                                                                         |
| Reconnect needed | The platform no longer accepts Acme's access. Nothing goes out on the channel until you reconnect it. |
| Turned off       | Nothing goes out on the channel.                                                                      |

A channel whose access cannot be renewed also shows the date by which it must be reconnected, and its workspace's owners and admins are told ahead of that date.

## Keep channels connected [#keep-connected]

Acme renews each account's access on its own before it runs out: it refreshes a Bluesky session a month before it would lapse, and X access, which lasts two hours, before it ends. Mastodon access does not expire until someone revokes it. LinkedIn access cannot be renewed: reconnect the channel every 60 days, as above.

When a platform refuses Acme's access while Acme posts, Acme renews the access where the platform allows it, and tries once more. When the platform refuses again, or the access cannot be renewed, for example because the account's owner revoked it, the channel turns to **Reconnect needed**, and the workspace's owners and admins are told once, under the bell and by email unless they turned that email off (see [Notifications](/notifications)). When a platform does not answer, Acme tries again later and leaves the channel as it is.

## What Acme posts [#posting]

At a post's time, Acme posts it as the channel's account, with its text on that channel and its images and videos, each with its alt text when it has one. [Images and videos](/media#platforms) lists what each platform accepts.

- **Mastodon**: Acme posts on the account's server, with the account's default visibility. The server turns mentions, hashtags and links into links itself, and may process a video before the post can show it.
- **Bluesky**: Acme posts on the server that holds the account. Links and hashtags become links; mentions stay plain text for now. Videos go through Bluesky's video service, which processes them before the post goes out.
- **LinkedIn**: Acme posts publicly, as the member. Hashtags become LinkedIn hashtags; mentions stay plain text. LinkedIn processes a video before the post goes out.
- **X**: Acme posts as the account. X turns mentions, hashtags and links into links itself, and processes a video before the post goes out.

## Reconnect [#reconnect]

Choose **Reconnect** next to the channel, then sign in on the platform with the same account. Signing in with another account is refused: to post to it, connect it as a new channel.

## Disconnect [#disconnect]

Choose **Disconnect** next to the channel and confirm. Acme deletes the tokens it holds for the account and posts nothing more to it. You can connect it again later.

Disconnecting does not tell the platform. To remove Acme's access there too, revoke it in the account's settings on the platform.

## When connecting fails [#errors]

| Message                                                          | What to do                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| This server is not set up to connect accounts of this platform   | Ask the instance's administrator: the server lacks a setting the platform needs. |
| This is not the address of a server                              | Check the Mastodon server's address, for example `mastodon.social`.              |
| Acme only connects to servers on the public internet, over HTTPS | The server is private, or does not answer over HTTPS.                            |
| No account was found for this handle                             | Check the Bluesky handle.                                                        |
| This connection expired or was started elsewhere                 | Start again from the workspace's channels, in this tab.                          |
| The connection was cancelled on the platform                     | You, or the platform, refused. Start again if you meant to connect.              |
| You signed in to another account than this channel's             | Reconnect with the channel's own account.                                        |
| The platform did not answer                                      | Try again in a moment.                                                           |
