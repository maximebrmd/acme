Acme tells the members who can act on it when something happens to a post or a channel. Every notification appears in Acme, under the bell in the header, and some also come by email.

## What you hear about [#events]

| Notification                       | When                                                                                                       | Who hears it                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| A post went out                    | The post is live on one of its channels.                                                                   | Members who may schedule posts: owners, admins and editors |
| A post did not go out              | The post failed on one of its channels, for the reason below.                                              | The same                                                   |
| A post may have gone out           | Acme could not tell whether a channel published it. Check the platform before you post it again.           | The same                                                   |
| A channel must be reconnected      | The platform no longer accepts Acme's access: nothing goes out on the channel until someone reconnects it. | Members who may connect channels: owners and admins        |
| A channel must be reconnected soon | The channel's access cannot be renewed, and ends on the date the notification gives.                       | The same                                                   |

Each notification about a post names one channel, so a post on three channels can bring three. A cancelled post brings none.

Why a post did not go out:

| Reason                                                                         | What to do                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The platform refused its text or its format                                    | Check the text against the platform's rules, and that the channel is still connected and on, then schedule it in a new post. |
| The platform refused one of its images or videos                               | Check the media against [what the platform accepts](/media#platforms).                                                       |
| Acme was unavailable at the scheduled time, and the post came too late to send | The server was down past the post's time. Schedule it in a new post.                                                         |
| The platform kept turning requests away, and Acme stopped trying               | The platform limited how often Acme posts. Schedule it later, in a new post.                                                 |
| The account's authorization stopped working                                    | [Reconnect](/channels#reconnect) the channel, then schedule it in a new post.                                                |
| The platform could not be reached                                              | Acme tried for over two hours. Schedule it in a new post once the platform answers.                                          |

## The bell and the notifications page [#inbox]

The bell in the header shows how many notifications you have not read, across all your workspaces. It checks every 30 seconds while the tab is open, and as soon as you come back to it, and screen readers hear when new ones arrive.

Choose the bell to open **Notifications**, newest first. Each one says what happened, in which workspace and on which channel, and links to the post or to the workspace's channels. Choose **Mark as read** on one, or **Mark all as read**, and **Show more** for older ones. Read notifications stay in the list. Times are in your browser's time zone.

You see the notifications of workspaces you have left too, under "a workspace you left".

## By email [#email]

From **Notifications**, choose **Email preferences** to pick which notifications also reach you by email. Each choice is saved as you change it, and applies in all your workspaces.

| Email me when                                                | Until you change it |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| A post did not go out                                        | On                  |
| A post may have gone out, and needs checking on the platform | On                  |
| A channel must be reconnected, now or soon                   | On                  |
| A post went out                                              | Off                 |

Emails are written in your language. When the mail server turns an email away for now, Acme tries again for about five hours. An email waiting to go out is dropped if you leave the workspace, or turn that email off, in the meantime.

## From the API [#api]

Your notifications and preferences belong to you, not to a workspace, so they live under `/api/me`:

- `GET /api/me/notices` lists them, newest first, 20 a page, and `GET /api/me/notices/unread` counts the unread ones;
- `POST /api/me/notices/read` marks up to 100 of them read, and `POST /api/me/notices/read-all` marks them all;
- `GET` and `PATCH /api/me/notification-preferences` read and change which ones are emailed.

Each notification is data, such as a post's id, a channel and a reason, for the client to write in its reader's language. These endpoints take a session or an OAuth token that is not bound to one workspace, such as the command line's. API keys, which belong to a workspace, and MCP clients, which act in one workspace, get 403. The [API reference](/reference) lists their parameters.
