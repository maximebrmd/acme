Acme is a social media scheduling platform. "Acme" is a working name.

<Callout type="warning" title="Early development">
  Acme is being built in the open and has not been released yet. You can already
  create workspaces, connect Mastodon and Bluesky accounts, write posts with
  images and videos, schedule them on a calendar and have them published, hear
  how each one went, see how they did on their platforms, and ask a writing
  assistant for help. These pages describe what works today, and grow as
  features land.
</Callout>

## What Acme does [#what-it-does]

- **Write once.** Draft a post in one editor, then give it its own text on each channel where it needs to differ. Counters show how much room each platform leaves.
- **Schedule across platforms.** Pick the accounts and the time. Acme publishes each post when it is due, in the time zone you scheduled it in, daylight saving included.
- **See results.** Acme reads back each post's likes, reposts, replies and quotes for a month, so you can see what worked in one place.
- **Work as a team.** Workspaces have members and roles, so several people can write, review and schedule together.

Acme never retries a post blindly: when a platform does not confirm whether a post went out, Acme asks again only in ways that cannot post it twice. If that still settles nothing, it marks the post unconfirmed and asks you to check the platform before posting again.

## Guides [#guides]

- [Connect channels](/channels): add the social accounts a workspace posts to.
- [Schedule posts](/scheduling): write a post, pick its channels and its time, move it on the calendar, and ask the writing assistant for help.
- [Images and videos](/media): attach media to a post, and see what each platform accepts.
- [Notifications](/notifications): hear how posts went and which channels need reconnecting, in the app and by email.
- [Analytics](/analytics): see how posts did, by channel and day, and which did best.
- [Command line](/cli): sign in from a terminal with `acme login`, and check who you are with `acme whoami`.
- [Connect an MCP client](/mcp): let an AI assistant read your workspace.

## Self-hostable [#self-hostable]

Every outside service Acme relies on has a self-hostable default. A self-hosted instance needs Postgres, an S3-compatible store and a way to send email; billing, captcha, AI and telemetry are optional. [Self-hosting](/self-hosting) explains how to run it, and [Environment variables](/environment-variables) lists every setting.

## Fair source [#fair-source]

The source is public under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-ALv2). You may use, modify and self-host Acme for any purpose except offering it to others as a competing commercial product or service, and each release also becomes available under the Apache License 2.0 two years after it ships. That is why Acme calls itself fair source rather than open source.
