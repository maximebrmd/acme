## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you (Article 15);
- have it corrected (Article 16);
- have it erased (Article 17): [Data deletion](/data-deletion/) explains how;
- restrict its processing (Article 18);
- receive it in a structured, machine-readable format (Article 20);
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21);
- withdraw your consent at any time where processing relies on it, without affecting the processing that came before (Article 7(3)).

Closing your account erases your data at once, except the identification data above, which French law requires us to keep (Article 17(3)(b)); your copy of your data lists it, and you can still ask for it by writing to privacy@peal.social. You can download a copy of your data at any time from your account settings, or with the command line; we email you each time a copy is downloaded, so a copy someone else took does not go unnoticed. To exercise these rights, write to privacy@peal.social. The GDPR requires an answer within one month, which can be extended in some cases (Article 12(3)).
