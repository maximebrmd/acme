## Personal data we process

These categories follow the service as it is designed. [Confirm each one against the released service.]

- **Account data**: your name, email address and sign-in details. [List the sign-in methods offered.]
- **Organisation data**: the organisations you belong to, your role in each, and the invitations you send or receive.
- **Connected accounts**: the social media accounts you connect, their identifiers on each platform, and the access tokens the platforms issue to Peal.
- **Content**: the posts, media and schedules you create, the publication history of each post, and how each post was created and scheduled (in the app, with an API key or through an application you connected, and by which member); per organisation and month, how many posts went out on X, and how many held a link.
- **Performance data**: the metrics the platforms report for your posts.
- **Webhooks**: the addresses an organisation has Peal call when something happens in it, and the log of each delivery (the event's identifiers, states and times, never what a post says, and how the receiving server answered).
- **Billing data**: your plan, subscription status and invoices; each order placed before paying, with the consent given and the version of its words; the trial each account and organisation started; each withdrawal and what it refunded; each request to cancel a contract or to withdraw from it, with the date and time we received it and what became of it, and, when it came through the form that needs no account, what you wrote there (your name, the email address for the acknowledgement, the contract, the day wanted). Payments are handled by [Payment provider]. [State which payment details, if any, we receive.]
- **Technical data**: IP addresses, browser details and logs produced when you use the service, and, for each connection that creates, changes or deletes content, its time, IP address and protocol.
- **Communications**: the messages you send us and the emails we send you.
- **Review data**: when a member sends someone a review link to a post, what that person, who needs no account, gives on the link's page: the name they choose, the comments they write, what they decide (an approval, a request for changes) and when. We take no email address from a reviewer, and send them nothing. Members' comments and decisions are kept with their account. We process a reviewer's data for the organisation that sent the link. ## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you (Article 15);
- have it corrected (Article 16);
- have it erased (Article 17): [Data deletion](/data-deletion/) explains how;
- restrict its processing (Article 18);
- receive it in a structured, machine-readable format (Article 20);
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21);
- withdraw your consent at any time where processing relies on it, without affecting the processing that came before (Article 7(3)).

Closing your account erases your data at once, except the identification data above, which French law requires us to keep (Article 17(3)(b)); your copy of your data lists it, and you can still ask for it by writing to privacy@peal.social. You can download a copy of your data at any time from your account settings, or with the command line; we email you each time a copy is downloaded, so a copy someone else took does not go unnoticed. To exercise these rights, write to privacy@peal.social. The GDPR requires an answer within one month, which can be extended in some cases (Article 12(3)).
